Where Quantum Matters

LevLab explores the new forms of quantum matter that can be created far from equilibrium—and how we can use them for devices.

Learn about our research

Our research

Across three experimental platforms, LevLab explores uncharted regimes of quantum matter using ultracold atoms, quantum optics, and condensed matter physics.

Dy multimode cQED

Topological pumping of one-dimensional dysprosium gases meets cavity photon-mediated interactions.

Explore Dysprosium →

Quantum-optical spin glass

Rubidium atoms wired together by photons realize spin glasses and quantum neural networks.

Explore Rubidium →

CavMat: Materials in Cavities

A cryogenic instrument couples two-dimensional quantum materials to a multimode optical cavity.
