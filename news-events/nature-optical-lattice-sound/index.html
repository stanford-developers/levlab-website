Nature: Optical lattice with sound

Our paper has appeared in Nature regarding the creation of the first optical lattice with sound vibrations---phonons.  This opens many new directions in the field of quantum simulation of quantum many-body systems.  Read more in Stanford News and APS Physics Magazine.
